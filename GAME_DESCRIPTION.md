# Aether’s Trial — Windborne

## Short description

Aether’s Trial: Windborne is a single-player, third-person fantasy adventure about finding purpose in a world you cannot remember. Explore a bright valley, meet its people, collect lost wind sigils, and fight elemental creatures with sword combos and Pyro abilities. With a floating companion beside you, face two ancient guardians and help restore the winds that once protected the valley.

## Full description

You wake on a cliff above Windrise Valley with no memory of how you arrived. Below you lie a lakeside town, vineyards, wooded paths, and ancient ruins. A small floating companion stays close as you search for answers.

Aether’s Trial: Windborne is a single-player 3D exploration and action-RPG prototype. Travel on foot, dash, jump, or glide through a compact fantasy valley. Meet residents at work, discover six landmarks, open treasure chests, and activate waypoints that let you rest and return to places you have found.

Lyra, keeper of the winds, asks you to recover scattered wind sigils. Along the way, fight through eight enemy camps using sword combos, Pyro skills, and elemental bursts. Read each enemy’s warning, dodge its attack, and choose a counter: flank a shield, sidestep an arrow, or escape a mage’s spell circle.

Two combat trials stand between the valley and quieter skies. Overcome their guardians, grow stronger through experience and loot, and give a traveler without a past a reason to protect the people around him.

## What players can do

- **Explore six landmarks:** Windrise Overlook, Mondstadt, Cider Lake, Windrise, Dawn Winery, and Stormterror Ruins.
- **Meet nine residents:** speak with Lyra and encounter people farming, selling produce, fishing, smithing, guarding, and making deliveries.
- **Challenge eight furnished camps:** 24 roaming enemies include elemental slimes, masked raiders, shield wardens, archers, mages, and ruin sentinels.
- **Use movement and timing:** combine sword attacks, Pyro, bursts, dashes, jumps, and gliding. Pyro against a Hydro target triggers Vaporize for increased damage.
- **Complete two trials:** defeat the field guardian, unlock the northern trial, and confront the Storm Drake.
- **Keep exploring:** discover eight valley chests and six wind sigils, activate waypoints, and resume a journey saved on the same browser and device.

## Story in one sentence

A traveler who has lost his memories helps Lyra reconnect the valley’s silent wind shrines, discovering that choosing whom to protect matters as much as remembering who he was.

## Presentation notes

This is an independent, fan-made prototype with anime-inspired scenery and original procedural interpretations of familiar fantasy characters and landmarks. Its presentation draws inspiration from Genshin Impact and Shadow Fight; it is not an official installment of either series.

The game is bundled for local play and does not require a cloud GPU or a game account. Browser verification uses a local server; direct opening of the self-contained HTML file remains a manual compatibility check. Working residents use simple authored routines, and the world is a compact valley rather than a production-scale open world. The sigil quest suggests the story’s route; defeating the first trial is the implemented requirement for unlocking the second.
